CARE & DETAILS

Made carefully. Made to last.

Every piece is created with natural materials and careful handwork. Here is what you need to know before it arrives at your home.

Materials

Handcrafted using natural clay, responsibly sourced wood, metal, and natural fibres. Slight variations in tone, grain, and texture are part of each handmade piece.

Care

Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid harsh cleaners, direct heat, and prolonged moisture. Keep wooden and fibre pieces away from direct sunlight to preserve their natural finish.

Shipping & Returns

Orders are carefully packed and dispatched within 3–5 working days. Made-to-order pieces may require additional production time. Returns are accepted within 7 days for unused items in original packaging.
