Contact support
How can I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking link. You can also contact us with your order number for an update.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, we ship across India. Shipping charges and delivery time may vary based on your location, product size, and the artisan’s making process.

Can I return or exchange a handmade piece?

Because many pieces are handmade or made to order, return and exchange options may vary by product. Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery if your item arrives damaged or incorrect.

Can I request a custom commission?

Yes. Share your idea, preferred materials, size, colour palette, budget, and timeline through the enquiry form. Our team will connect you with a suitable artisan.

How can I collaborate with Hastakala?

We welcome collaborations with designers, retailers, hotels, institutions, and creative brands. Send us a short introduction and project details through the contact form.

Do you offer studio visits or workshops?

Yes. You can visit the studio to explore handmade collections and meet the artisans. For group visits, workshops, or a scheduled artisan appointment, please contact us in advance.
